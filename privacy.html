At DailyPlanner ("we", "us", "our"), your privacy is our absolute top priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and website.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the information absolutely necessary to provide you with the best planning experience:

  • Account Information: If you create an account, we may collect your email address and name.
  • Usage Data: We collect anonymous, aggregated data on how users interact with the app to improve our AI scheduling algorithms.
  • Task Data: Your tasks, routines, and calendar entries are processed securely to generate your daily plan.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly used to:

  • Provide and maintain the DailyPlanner service.
  • Personalize your AI-generated schedules and timetables.
  • Improve our algorithms and app functionality.
  • Communicate with you regarding updates or support inquiries.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard AES-256 encryption security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. We never sell your personal data to third parties. Your calendar is your business.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, export, or permanently delete your personal information at any time directly through the app settings or by contacting our support team.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and pushing an alert to your app.
